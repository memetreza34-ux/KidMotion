# KidMotion Character Bible

These two characters are the approved KidMotion master identities.

# IMPORTANT CURRENT OVERRIDE
The exact prompts below describe the ORIGINAL MASTER REFERENCE LOOK used to lock face, age, hair, skin tone, proportions and 3D style.

They do NOT mean the base sports outfit must be used in every episode.

Current production rule:
- one episode chooses exactly ONE of these two characters
- that character stays the same from Cover to Outro
- the character may wear an episode-specific themed outfit, e.g. football, basketball, tennis, winter or space
- once an episode outfit is chosen, that outfit stays consistent throughout the episode
- never switch between Boy and Girl inside one normal episode

`PROJECT_BRAIN.md` and `gehirn/CHARACTERS.md` control the current episode/outfit rules.

## Boy — exact approved MASTER reference prompt

```text
Create a premium 3D animated character concept image for an original kids fitness brand called KidMotion.

Show ONE original main character standing alone on a clean light studio background.

CHARACTER – BOY:
A friendly athletic boy around 10 years old, child-appropriate body proportions, slim and active, not muscular, medium warm skin tone, short slightly textured dark brown hair, large expressive brown eyes, soft rounded facial features, confident friendly smile.

He wears a modern sporty MASTER REFERENCE outfit:
bright blue athletic T-shirt with subtle green accents,
dark navy athletic shorts,
white crew socks,
clean blue-and-white sneakers.

His design should feel energetic, adventurous, sporty and highly recognizable.

STYLE:
High-end stylized 3D animation, polished family entertainment quality, original character design, soft cinematic studio lighting, clean shapes, expressive face, realistic enough body proportions for demonstrating fitness and sports poses, slightly stylized larger eyes but NOT oversized head, premium materials, soft skin shading, detailed hair, colorful but tasteful sportswear.

IMPORTANT IDENTITY LOCK:
Preserve exactly this face, age appearance, hairstyle, skin tone, body proportions, eye style and overall rendering direction.
Do NOT make him younger.
Do NOT make him more cartoonish.
Do NOT enlarge his head.
Do NOT simplify the body proportions.
Do NOT redesign the face.

For this MASTER REFERENCE image, keep the listed outfit colors.
Episode-specific prompts may later replace the outfit with a themed KidMotion outfit while keeping the same identity.

No logos from existing brands.
No copyrighted characters.
No recognizable franchise resemblance.
No text.

Full body visible from head to shoes.
Front-facing neutral athletic standing pose.
Clean light studio background.
Vertical 4:5 composition.
```

## Locked consistency rules

Use the master images as visual references whenever possible.

Never casually alter:
- facial identity
- hairstyle
- age appearance
- skin tone
- body proportions
- eye style
- overall 3D rendering direction

Outfit rule:
- base/master outfit is a reference, not a permanent outfit for every episode
- episode-themed outfit may change between videos
- episode outfit must remain locked within that video

The objective is stable recurring mascots, not a new interpretation in every generation.
