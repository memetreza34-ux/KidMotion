# KM-002 — Basketball Hold Workout for Kids

Date: 2026-08-17

## Video title
**5 Minute Basketball Workout for Kids | 12 Easy Hold Poses**

## Core concept
A basketball-themed KidMotion follow-along workout built ONLY from static poses that are immediately understandable from one image.

### Hard pose rule
Every exercise must pass the EIN-BILD-KLARHEITSTEST:
- one static end pose only
- full body visible
- clear arm/leg separation
- readable silhouette
- no movement direction needed
- no dribbling, shooting, jumping, running or passing motion
- if Google Flow cannot show the pose clearly after two good attempts, replace the pose

## Character
Exactly ONE recurring KidMotion girl for the whole episode.

Locked identity:
- around 10 years old
- medium warm skin
- long dark-brown hair in a sporty high ponytail
- large expressive brown eyes
- soft rounded facial features
- cheerful/confident expression
- slim active child-appropriate proportions
- premium stylized 3D family-entertainment look

Locked episode outfit/world:
- original purple basketball jersey with aqua accents
- dark navy basketball shorts
- white/purple basketball shoes
- bright indoor basketball gym
- polished wooden court
- generic hoop in background when composition allows
- no real team, league, sponsor or sports-brand logos
- no other people
