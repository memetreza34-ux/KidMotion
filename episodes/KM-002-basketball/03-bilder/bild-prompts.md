# KM-002 — Google Flow Image Prompts

## HARD RULES
- Exactly ONE recurring character for the complete episode: the approved KidMotion GIRL.
- `Bild 01.png` is the PRIMARY continuity reference for `Bild 02.png` through `Bild 16.png`.
- Same face, ponytail, age appearance, skin tone, proportions, purple/aqua basketball outfit, shoes, gym and premium 3D style throughout.
- No second child, coach, spectator or background person.
- 16:9 landscape, 1920x1080 target.
- Generate exactly ONE image at a time, verify it, rename it immediately, then continue.

## EIN-BILD-KLARHEITSTEST
Every exercise must be immediately understandable from one still image.

Before accepting any exercise image, check:
1. full body visible
2. arms and legs clearly separated
3. important joints visible
4. silhouette is clear even without text
5. pose has one static end position
6. no movement direction or animation is required to understand it
7. no awkward anatomy or hidden limbs

If the pose still looks unclear after two good generations, REPLACE THE POSE instead of adding more explanation.

## Exercise text responsibility
For `Bild 04.png` through `Bild 15.png`, Google Flow itself creates:
- exercise title at BOTTOM-LEFT
- short instruction at TOP-RIGHT inside the KidMotion white cloud/info bubble

Keep TOP-LEFT free for Remotion timer.
Keep the BOTTOM EDGE free for Remotion progress.

Do NOT generate:
- subtitles
- transcript bars
- timer
- progress bar
- duplicate title
- duplicate instruction
