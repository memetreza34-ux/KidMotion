# KM-002 — Audio

## FINAL REQUIRED FILES
A final KM-002 render requires exactly:
- `Voice 01.mp3`
- `bg-music.mp3`

Do not create Voice 02, Voice 03 or exercise Voice files.

## Voice 01
`Voice 01.mp3` is ONE continuous opening Voice.

It starts at 0s and may continue across Intro → Safety → LET'S BEGIN.
Target: about 11–14 seconds.
It MUST finish before the first exercise starts at 14s.

Copy-ready text:

Hi, KidMotion friends! It’s basketball time! Today we have twelve easy poses, twenty seconds each. Find some space, make sure you can move safely, and stop if anything hurts. Get ready, stay strong, and let’s begin!

After the opening there is no spoken Voice anywhere in the workout.

## Background music
`bg-music.mp3` is REQUIRED for final production.

Music must be:
- licensed for the intended publishing platforms
- instrumental
- playful / sporty / child-friendly
- no vocals
- no spoken samples

Remotion behavior:
- low volume under Voice 01
- slightly louder after 14s
- loop continuously through the full 5:13 workout
- still audible around the middle and near the end

The rejected KM-002 test MP4 became digitally silent after about 8 seconds. That is a hard failure and must not happen again.

Do not reuse music from another episode unless its license permits reuse and it is intentionally copied into this episode folder.
