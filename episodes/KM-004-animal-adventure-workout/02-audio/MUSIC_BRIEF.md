# KM-004 Music Brief

Use one upbeat, playful kids-fitness electro-pop instrumental.

Requirements:
- 120–130 BPM
- bright, adventurous, energetic, friendly
- light electronic drums, claps, playful synths, warm bass
- subtle jungle/adventure flavor is okay, but no distracting animal sound effects
- no vocals, no spoken words
- no dark trap, aggressive EDM, scary sounds, or babyish nursery-music feel
- seamless or clean loop preferred
- commercial YouTube use must be allowed
- music stays low under the opening voice, then becomes noticeably more energetic after LET'S BEGIN

Final file name: `bg-music.mp3`
