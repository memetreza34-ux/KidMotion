# KM-005 Music Brief

Target: upbeat playful space-fitness electro-pop instrumental, 120–130 BPM.

Requirements:
- bright, energetic, futuristic but friendly
- clean synth pulse, light electronic drums, playful space accents
- no vocals
- no dark trap, aggressive EDM or cinematic danger mood
- no nursery-song/baby feel
- commercial YouTube usage must be allowed
- seamless or nearly seamless loop preferred
- lower under the 14-second opening voice, then noticeably more energetic for the workout
