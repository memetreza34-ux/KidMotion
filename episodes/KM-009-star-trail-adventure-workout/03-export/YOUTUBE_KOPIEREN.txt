TITLE
5 Minute Adventure Workout for Kids | Star Trail Freeze Challenge

DESCRIPTION
Follow twelve painted stars through a friendly park! Try safe freeze poses: reach, stretch, balance, squat gently and celebrate at the finish. No equipment needed. Make some space, move gently and stop if anything hurts. Keep toes on the ground if balance feels tricky.

12 fun exercises, short GET READY breaks and a little park mission to complete.
