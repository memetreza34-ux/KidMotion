5 Minute Pirate Treasure Workout for Kids | 12 Fun Freeze Moves

DESCRIPTION
Follow a colorful treasure map through a friendly pirate-themed playground! Try twelve fun, gentle freeze poses: reaching, stretching, balancing, squatting and easy side steps. No equipment needed.

Make a safe open space. Keep toes grounded when balance feels tricky and stop if anything hurts. Twelve 20-second movement poses plus short GET READY breaks.
