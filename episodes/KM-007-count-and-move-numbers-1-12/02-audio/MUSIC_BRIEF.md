# KM-007 Music Brief

Target: upbeat playful educational kids-fitness instrumental, 120–130 BPM.

Requirements:
- bright, curious and energetic
- clean pop drums, playful marimba/pluck sounds, warm synth bass and light hand-clap accents
- clear steady pulse that supports counting
- no vocals
- no spoken counting
- no dark/aggressive EDM or trap
- no nursery-song/baby feel
- commercial YouTube usage must be allowed
- seamless or nearly seamless loop preferred
- lower under the 14-second opening voice, stronger after the opening
