# KM-006 Music Brief

Target: upbeat playful jungle-adventure kids-fitness instrumental, 120–130 BPM.

Requirements:
- bright, energetic and adventurous
- playful percussion, light marimba/wooden accents, clean pop drums and warm synth bass
- subtle jungle atmosphere without animal calls dominating the track
- no vocals
- no dark cinematic danger mood
- no aggressive EDM
- no nursery-song/baby feel
- commercial YouTube usage must be allowed
- seamless or nearly seamless loop preferred
- lower under the 14-second opening voice, then noticeably more energetic for the workout
